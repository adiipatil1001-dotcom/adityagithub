Our Story

Born from the Spirit of Kolhapur

Kolhapur Airlines was founded with a single vision: to put Kolhapur on the aviation map of India. Rooted in the rich heritage of the city of warriors, we carry the same spirit of courage, pride, and excellence in every flight we operate.

From our humble beginnings with two aircraft and three routes, we have grown into a regional powerhouse connecting Maharashtra's cultural capital to major Indian cities with comfort and reliability.
